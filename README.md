## 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm a **Computer Science graduate (S.Kom)** from **Universitas Lambung Mangkurat**, currently working as **Professional Staff** at **Disbudporapar Kota Banjarmasin** — the city's department for culture, youth, sports, and tourism.

Most of my work is backend engineering: building systems for government offices and helping digitize historical records that used to just sit in old file cabinets. It's the kind of work that directly affects how public services run, which I find more meaningful than most.
